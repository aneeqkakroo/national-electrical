Testing & EICR

What your electrical report means

An Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) checks wiring, sockets, protective devices and earthing against the current wiring regulations. Anything we find is given one of four codes.

C1
Danger presentRisk of injury. We make it safe straight away, before we leave.
C2
Potentially dangerousNeeds urgent repair. The report is unsatisfactory until it's fixed.
FI
Further investigationSomething needs checking more closely without delay.
C3
Improvement recommendedNot dangerous, but bringing it up to date would improve safety.

How often to test

Maximum time between inspections, based on IET guidance.

Rented homes (legal requirement)5 years
Owner-occupied homes10 years
Offices, shops & commercial5 years
Industrial premises3 years
Swimming pools & saunas1 year
Construction sites3 months

Also when you buy or sell a home, or at a change of tenancy.
