Testing & inspection

Landlord electrical safety certificates

Private landlords in England must have the electrical installation in rented homes inspected and tested at least every five years. We make it simple: book the EICR, we do the test, fix anything that needs fixing and send you the paperwork.

What the law requires

Under the Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020, you must have the installation tested at least every five years, give a copy of the report to existing tenants within 28 days, give it to new tenants before they move in, and supply it to the local council within seven days if they ask.

If remedial work is needed

Any C1, C2 or FI items must be put right within 28 days, or sooner if the report says so. You then need written confirmation that the work is complete. We provide that confirmation with a certificate for the work.

Understanding your report

Every issue on an EICR is given a code. Here's what they mean.

C1
Danger presentRisk of injury. We make it safe straight away, before we leave.
C2
Potentially dangerousNeeds urgent repair. The report is unsatisfactory until it's fixed.
FI
Further investigationSomething needs checking more closely without delay.
C3
Improvement recommendedNot dangerous, but bringing it up to date would improve safety.

How often to test

Maximum time between inspections, based on IET guidance.

Rented homes (legal requirement)5 years
Owner-occupied homes10 years
Offices, shops & commercial5 years
Industrial premises3 years
Swimming pools & saunas1 year
Construction sites3 months

Also when you buy or sell a home, or at a change of tenancy.

HMOs often need fire alarm systems and emergency lighting too, depending on size and your council's licensing conditions. We can check and quote for everything in one visit.
