Repairs & emergencies

Emergency electrician

If you've lost power, can smell burning or have exposed or damaged wiring, call us. An electrician will come out to make the installation safe and get the power back on where possible.

Get a free quoteCall 07344 988182

What's included

  • Complete or partial power loss
  • RCD or breaker that won't reset
  • Burning smell or scorch marks
  • Exposed or damaged wiring
  • Water leaks near electrics
  • Storm and flood damage
  • Make-safe after an accident
  • Reports for insurers
Power cut in the street?If your neighbours have also lost power, it's probably a supply fault. Call 105, the free national power cut number, before calling an electrician.

What to do while you wait

If it's safe, switch off at the main switch on your consumer unit. Don't touch damaged cables or anything wet. If the problem is with the meter or the supply cable outside, call your network operator on 105.
