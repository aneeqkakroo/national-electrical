Testing & inspection

EICR and periodic inspection

An Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) is a thorough check of your fixed wiring: circuits, sockets, switches, protective devices, earthing and bonding. It tells you whether the installation is safe to keep using.

Get a free quoteCall 07344 988182

What's included

  • Visual inspection of the whole installation
  • Testing of every circuit
  • RCD and protective device tests
  • Earthing and bonding checks
  • Clear coded report (C1, C2, C3, FI)
  • Photos of any issues found
  • Fixed quote for any remedial work
  • Report emailed within 48 hours

When you need one

When you buy or sell a property, before letting it, every five years for rented homes and commercial premises, every ten years for owner-occupied homes, or if you're worried about the condition of the wiring.

Satisfactory or unsatisfactory

If any C1, C2 or FI codes are found the report is unsatisfactory. We'll explain each item in plain English, give you a fixed price to put it right, and issue the paperwork once it's done.

Understanding your report

Every issue on an EICR is given a code. Here's what they mean.

C1
Danger presentRisk of injury. We make it safe straight away, before we leave.
C2
Potentially dangerousNeeds urgent repair. The report is unsatisfactory until it's fixed.
FI
Further investigationSomething needs checking more closely without delay.
C3
Improvement recommendedNot dangerous, but bringing it up to date would improve safety.

How often to test

Maximum time between inspections, based on IET guidance.

Rented homes (legal requirement)5 years
Owner-occupied homes10 years
Offices, shops & commercial5 years
Industrial premises3 years
Swimming pools & saunas1 year
Construction sites3 months

Also when you buy or sell a home, or at a change of tenancy.

Questions

How long does an EICR take?

A typical flat takes two to three hours and a three-bedroom house three to four hours. The power will be off at times during testing.

Is an EICR the same as a landlord safety certificate?

Yes. 'Landlord electrical safety certificate' is the common name for an EICR on a rented property.
