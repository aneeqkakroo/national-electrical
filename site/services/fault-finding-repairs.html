Repairs & emergencies

Electrical fault finding and repairs

A tripping RCD, a dead socket or lights that flicker are signs that something is wrong. We trace the fault with the right test equipment, explain what we've found and fix it.

Get a free quoteCall 07344 988182

What's included

  • Tripping RCDs and breakers
  • Dead sockets and circuits
  • Flickering or failed lighting
  • Burning smells and scorched fittings
  • Damaged cables and accessories
  • Faulty appliances and connections
  • Water and storm damage
  • Insurance reports

How we find faults

We isolate circuits one at a time and use insulation resistance testing to narrow down the fault, rather than guessing and replacing parts. That usually means a quicker, cheaper fix.

Questions

My RCD trips when it rains. Why?

Water is getting into an outdoor circuit, such as a garden socket, outside light or garage supply. Avoid using it and call us to trace and fix it.

Is a burning smell an emergency?

Yes. Switch off at the consumer unit if it's safe to do so and call us straight away on the emergency line.
