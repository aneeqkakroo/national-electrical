One contractor for the whole building

From the main switch to the last LED panel, we handle design, installation, testing and certification. You get one point of contact and one set of documents for your building file.

Lower running costs

Replacing old fluorescent fittings with LED panels and adding presence detection often pays for itself in two to three years. We'll give you a lighting survey with estimated savings.

Questions

Do you provide RAMS?

Yes. We provide risk assessments and method statements before work starts, and we're used to working on occupied sites.

Can you take over maintenance of our building?

Yes. We offer planned maintenance contracts that cover periodic testing, emergency lighting tests and reactive call-outs.
