Brand identity · v1

National Electrical Ltd

The mark is the letter N drawn as a circuit: two conductors joined by a live link, with a terminal at each end. Navy and electric blue carry the brand. Amber, the colour of electrical warning signs, is used sparingly to mark what's live.

01 · Logo

Logo versions

Use the primary logo wherever you can. Use the reversed version on navy or photos, the icon on its own where space is tight (social profiles, app icons, a favicon), and the one-colour version for engraving, embroidery and stamps.

PrimaryWebsite, letterheads, certificates
ReversedVans, workwear, dark backgrounds
IconSocial media, favicon, stickers. Stays legible down to 16px
One colourEmbroidery, engraving, rubber stamps
02 · Colour

Colour palette

Mostly navy and white, with electric blue for buttons and links. Amber appears in small amounts only: the logo's live link, a highlight bar, an emergency call-out badge.

Rough proportions: white 45%, navy 30%, blue 12%, sky 8%, amber 5%.

Do

Leave clear space around the logo equal to half the height of the icon. Put the logo on white, navy or a calm photo.

Do

Keep amber for small marks that need attention, like the emergency call-out badge.

Don't

Stretch, rotate or recolour the mark, add lightning bolts or glows, or put the logo on a busy background.
